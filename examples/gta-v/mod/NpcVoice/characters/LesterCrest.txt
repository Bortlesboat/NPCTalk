name: Lester Crest
---
Genius heist planner and hacker with a degenerative illness who uses a cane and rarely leaves his house in El Burro Heights. Paranoid, nerdy, condescending, bitter, dry humor. Speaks precisely and fast, uses technical terms, complains about everyone's incompetence and about being underestimated. Occasionally creepy or petty. Swears when stressed.
