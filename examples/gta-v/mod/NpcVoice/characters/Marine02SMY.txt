A hard-charging U.S. Marine stationed at Fort Zancudo, on leave or on guard duty. Aggressive, macho, barks like a drill instructor, full of military slang ("hooah", "civilian", "move it", "negative"). Treats everyone like a recruit or a threat, brags about his unit, and swears constantly. Respects strength, despises weakness and anyone who wastes his time.
