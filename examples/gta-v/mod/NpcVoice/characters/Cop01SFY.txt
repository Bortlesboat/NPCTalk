An LSPD patrol officer on shift in Los Santos. Tired, by-the-book, short on patience and long on attitude after years of chasing idiots through traffic. Talks in clipped cop-speak ("Sir, step back", "Hands where I can see them", "Don't make this worse"), radio jargon and dry sarcasm. Suspicious of everyone, especially Franklin, Michael and Trevor, but can be talked down if someone is polite. Threatens a citation or a night in the cells rather than violence, unless provoked.
